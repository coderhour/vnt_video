# Xiaohongshu now favors Vantee's long explainers

Yes, Vantee should post to Xiaohongshu, but as a measured four-to-eight-week test, not a full rollout. Four findings support that. First, AMC 8 in China is a parent-driven market of about **80,000 entrants a year**, and Xiaohongshu is where affluent, search-first parents gather. Second, the AMC content visible on Xiaohongshu is institution-run image-and-text (图文) lead generation, and no animated single-problem explainer series turned up, which is consistent with the user's own empty search. Third, since **May 26, 2026** Xiaohongshu has officially defaulted mid-long video to **horizontal (横屏)**, made **收藏 (saves), follows and long watch time core ranking signals**, and stretched recommendation of good mid-long video to **90 days**. Vantee's 6.5–8 minute 16:9 Mandarin explainers are exactly what that change rewards. Fourth, the rules allow an AI-voice channel as long as every post is declared as AI content and nothing points off the platform. WeChat Channels, by contrast, is described everywhere as friend-graph-first, and no source measures how far a channel with no friend network can reach. The honest caveat is that **no like, save, comment or follower numbers for any AMC content on Xiaohongshu could be collected**: search and note pages need a login, and the analytics vendors are paid. So this report can say the opening exists and the platform favors the format. It cannot say how many views to expect. The plan at the end is built to find out cheaply.

## A parent market served only by image posts and sales funnels

The audience is real but indirect. One AMC registration site reports that the 2025 AMC 8 had over 300,000 entrants worldwide and **close to 80,000 in China** ([amcclub.org.cn](https://amcclub.org.cn/news/4446/)). A training-site article dated August 2026 says domestic registrations for 2026 passed 80,000 ([jingsailian](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html)). Huxiu, a more neutral outlet, counts about **7,500 Beijing entrants** in 2025 ([Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4862663.html)). None of these figures comes from MAA, and the institutions behind most of them sell AMC courses. The contest is also getting younger, with the share of 4th and 5th graders said to rise every year ([jingsailian](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html)). That is because families use AMC 8 as a "hard signal" for middle-school entry. Beijing's 2025 admissions guidance flatly bans using "各类竞赛证书" (contest certificates of any kind) in compulsory-education admissions ([Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/local/20250418/287fb0ec4f634bd6b6770c4685f4c678/c.html)). Training brands still market AMC 8 as "小升初的必须项", a must-have for the move to middle school ([x-newedu](https://www.x-newedu.com/n/202607/64613.html)). The result is a contest that matters to parents but is officially frowned on, and that kind of talk moves into parent-to-parent communities. Xiaohongshu has one of the largest: an **88 million-strong active parenting (母婴) population** in 2023 ([Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1239923.html)), about 46% of users aged 30 or over, and 37.2% in tier-1 and new-tier-1 cities ([QuestMobile via Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1441257.html)). AMC 10 is a much smaller market in China. No China-specific numbers for it were found.

What Xiaohongshu indexes about AMC is almost entirely 图文 notes. Examples include a 犀牛 (Rhino) promo for "300道分类练习题" (300 categorized practice problems), a teacher's bilingual note that works one classic puzzle and ends with an AMC 8 challenge, and an Australian agency posting "AMC放榜" (results are out) ([xiaohongshu.com](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/mobile/question/2837913); [Qs School GROUP](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/user/profile/61b83ae4000000001000d928)). The big three AMC trainers, 翰林 (Hanlin), 犀牛 (Rhino) and 唯寻 (Weixun), charge roughly ¥300–500 a lesson by Hanlin's own self-serving comparison ([hanlin.com](https://www.hanlin.com/archives/1207350)). Their web articles follow a single template: contest news, cutoff analysis, prep plan, then a WeChat contact ([jingsailian](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html)). Mandarin single-problem videos do exist, but elsewhere. There are teacher whiteboard clips titled "某年AMC8第X题" (AMC 8 [year], problem X) on Douyin- or Haokan-style accounts surfaced by Baidu video search ([Baidu vsearch](https://m.baidu.com/vsearch/xsppage?pd=video_page&atn=vertical&dsp=iphone&resource_id=5021&from_sf=1&word=amc8%E5%8E%86%E5%B9%B4%E7%9C%9F%E9%A2%98%E5%85%8D%E8%B4%B9)). Bilibili has lecture series such as Po-Shen Loh's 25-part AMC 8 course ([Bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1T3411w7zG/)).

So the user's empty search is believable, and the likely reasons point to an opening rather than a dead zone. Institutions use Xiaohongshu to collect DMs and WeChat adds, and a complete, free animated solution works against that funnel. Animation is expensive for individual teachers. And Xiaohongshu's off-platform rules push creators who want links toward Douyin and Bilibili. Vantee wants none of those things on Xiaohongshu, so the gap fits its position. The risk on the other side is that the gap might mean low demand for the format, not unmet demand. Only a test can tell those apart. One more thing to check first: the evidence comes from indexed snippets, not the logged-in 视频 tab. A ten-minute manual search (see the checklist at the end) should confirm the gap before any production time goes in.

## Xiaohongshu's 2026 ranking changes reward 6–8 minute horizontal solutions

The earlier research notes disagree on format, and the newer official evidence settles it. One line of advice holds that Xiaohongshu is a vertical feed where 16:9 shows black bars, so only the 9:16 teasers belong there ([aiyongtech](https://www.aiyongtech.com/new/id10313)). That was the received wisdom until this year. At its RED 新生代创作盛典 on **May 26, 2026**, Xiaohongshu **made horizontal the default for 中长视频 (mid-long video)**. It also put comments on the same screen as the video, opened free 4K, named **收藏, 关注, 长时播放, 横屏 and 弹幕** (saves, follows, long watch time, horizontal, bullet comments) as core signals for mid-long video, and **extended mid-long recommendation to 90 days** ([Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103201938.html); [NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-05-27/4410421.html)). Reported results since: the exposure share of good mid-long video rose **5x in six months**, mid-long views grew 30%+ year over year, and nearly 100 million daily users watch it. The platform says it has raised the weight of saves, follows and watch time ([36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3931553397292419)). There is also a creator incentive plan offering up to 80,000 traffic coupons a month for completing update tasks ([36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3931553397292419)).

This matters because of what the repo actually holds. The Chinese explainers are **385–475 seconds long (6.5–8 minutes)**, longer than the 3–6 minutes in the brief. That puts them firmly in the mid-long tier the platform is promoting. "Over two minutes" is the bar used by Xiaohongshu's creator contest, but it is not a stated platform-wide definition. Third-party guides put the upload cap at 15 minutes ([riseton](https://www.riseton.com/yunying/5977.html)). One unofficial 2026 spec sheet says 5 minutes ([resouci](https://resouci.com/xiaohongshu-video-note-spec-2026/)). If 5 minutes were true it would block every explainer, so check the cap in the app first. The 30–40 s vertical shorts are a separate product competing in the short feed. The 2026 changes say nothing about how they perform. On Xiaohongshu the full explainer is probably the main product and the short the hook, which reverses the YouTube setup.

A save-weighted, search-driven platform suits a worked solution a parent wants to keep and show a child. Xiaohongshu's own 2024 search-trends report says **over 70% of monthly users search**, about six times a day, and a third search first when they open the app ([Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1359882.html)). A 90-day recommendation window plus search-led discovery favors evergreen, keyword-rich titles over trend timing. The widely quoted "CES" score (like 1, save 1, comment 4, share 4, follow 8) has no official source ([woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/6073503.html)). For mid-long video the 2026 official signals replace it. The only engagement benchmark found for knowledge content is from health education, not math. There, a 23 s video drew **32,000 saves on 54,000 likes**, roughly 60%, and grew the account from about 3,000 to 22,000 followers in two weeks ([36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3931553397292419)). Use the save-to-like ratio as the first sign of whether Xiaohongshu parents treat Vantee videos as reference material.

Packaging needs one Xiaohongshu-specific change. The current Chinese titles end in "数学思维讲解" (math-thinking explainer) with tags #数学思维 #趣味数学 #数学题, and none of them contains "AMC". That suits WeChat's forward-to-a-friend culture. It loses the search traffic of parents typing "AMC8". Because the Vantee quiz-bank problems are **original AMC-style problems, not past papers**, the titles must not say "真题" (real past-paper problem). Calling an original problem a past-paper problem would also run into the platform's 虚构/夸大 (fabrication and exaggeration) monitoring ([woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6124879.html)). Accurate phrasings such as "AMC8 同类题" (AMC 8–type problem) or "AMC8 几何练习" (AMC 8 geometry practice) keep the keyword without the claim.

## The rules allow AI narration but punish automation, off-platform hints and admissions claims

**AI labeling is mandatory and simple.** The CAC's 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》 (AI-generated content labeling measures) took effect **September 1, 2025**. It defines covered content to include **audio**, so Gemini TTS narration is in scope. Article 10 requires users who publish such content to "主动声明" (declare it) with the platform's labeling tool ([full text, Guangming](https://m.gmw.cn/2025-03/15/content_1303991695.htm)). Under Article 6, a declared post gets "可能为生成合成内容" (may be AI-generated). An undeclared post the platform detects gets "疑似生成合成内容" (suspected AI-generated) ([Guangming](https://m.gmw.cn/2025-03/15/content_1303991695.htm)). Remotion re-encodes the audio and Gemini is not a PRC-registered provider, so the MP4 almost certainly carries no machine-readable label. That leaves the creator's own declaration as the only compliant route. On Xiaohongshu it is 设置 → 内容类型声明 → 「笔记含AI合成内容」 (Settings → content type declaration → "note contains AI-synthesized content"), ticked on every post ([NetEase](https://www.163.com/dy/article/K86VAJIQ053144S4.html)). The April 2026 《AI 治理规则》 (AI governance rules) add graded penalties, from distribution limits to bans, for undeclared AI content. The same rules name compliant "科普教育" (popular-science education) among the AI content that gets traffic support ([Sohu/PConline](https://www.sohu.com/a/1015736769_223764); [ITHome](https://www.ithome.com/0/944/156.htm)). One reported official line says declaring does not reduce distribution, but it was said about virtual-human accounts ([ITHome](https://www.ithome.com/0/987/042.htm)). Whether a declared ordinary note gets less reach is officially unaddressed. The clear risk lies elsewhere. In March 2026 Xiaohongshu started acting against **AI 托管 (AI-hosted)** accounts, which post and interact automatically ([ITHome](https://www.ithome.com/0/927/689.htm)). It reported 1.2 million such accounts handled by May ([36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3931553397292419)). A 73-video backlog from one template is precisely what that enforcement looks for. So post by hand from the app or the official creator web page, never through scripts, vary titles and covers, and answer comments as a person.

**Decide whether the account is a brand or a persona.** The August 2026 rules require AI virtual-human accounts to say so on the profile and carry an "AI虚拟人" (AI virtual human) tag. Undeclared persona accounts reportedly risk throttling ([ITHome](https://www.ithome.com/0/987/042.htm); [ifeng](https://i.ifeng.com/c/8vONpzWJByU)). If the account presents as "Vantee the tutor" speaking in first person, use the tag. If it is a brand account whose videos feature a character, the per-note declaration plus a bio line such as "AI配音" (AI voice) is enough. A cheap on-screen "AI配音" in the opening frame matches the spirit of Article 4, though strictly that article binds the generating service, not the uploader.

**Off-platform hints are the likeliest way to get throttled.** The revised 《交易导流违规管理细则》 (rules on diverting transactions off-platform) took effect **September 18, 2026**. They ban contact details, other platforms' account names, links, QR codes, watermarks, mini-programs, directions to third-party sites or apps, and "implied or coded" guidance. They cover ordinary users' titles, text, images, videos, comments and DMs. Penalties run from warnings and traffic limits up to account bans ([NetEase](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L6MU30SF053144S4.html)). The repo's China cut already drops the domain, the link and the ".ai". On Xiaohongshu also keep "下载Vantee" (download Vantee), "App Store", "搜索Vantee" (search Vantee) and the classic education pattern "私信/评论区领资料" (DM or comment to get the materials) out of every surface. A logo or brand name alone is a gray area. The enterprise guide lets account names be based on an app, which suggests the name itself is tolerated ([enterprise account guide](https://dc.xhscdn.com/62f0c1cfe638eb13c67415419cd6d5c75985fa2b.pdf)). A call to go and get the app is what crosses the line. This also means Xiaohongshu will not send installs directly. Its value is audience and brand searches, which shows up indirectly.

**Education rules bind ads and claims, not free explainers.** No rule found bans free math 科普 or worked solutions. Advertising is a different matter. Shenzhen's 双减 (double-reduction) compliance guidance bans training ads aimed at K-12 students on online platforms, including disguised "思维训练班" (thinking-training classes) ([Shenzhen Justice Bureau](https://sf.sz.gov.cn/attachment/1/1715/1715454/12799812.pdf)). Agencies report that Xiaohongshu ads for education need a school-running permit (办学许可证) and that words like 保过 (guaranteed pass), 押题 (exam-question tipping) and 稳拿 (sure win) are banned ([xiao-ad](https://www.xiao-ad.com/jd/1195.html)). Paid boosting (薯条/聚光) of AMC prep is therefore effectively closed to Vantee, and growth must be organic. In copy, frame videos as problem-solving and thinking, never as a route to 小升初 (middle-school entry), awards or score gains. Keep minors off camera; the cartoon tutor makes that easy.

**Account type: start simple.** Ordinary accounts accept foreign phone numbers without Chinese ID verification ([KR-Asia](https://www.kr-asia.com/tiktok-refugees-a-mixed-blessing-for-chinese-app-xiaohongshu)). Enterprise blue-V verification officially accepts overseas companies using notarized registration documents. Names and bios are capped at 20 characters. An app-based name needs a Chinese software copyright certificate. The guide lists a non-refundable ¥300 annual review fee, and newer agency posts say ¥600 ([enterprise guide](https://dc.xhscdn.com/62f0c1cfe638eb13c67415419cd6d5c75985fa2b.pdf); [opp2](https://www.opp2.com/345254.html)). For a test, a personal or free creator account is enough. The biggest unknown sits here. An unverified report says accounts registered after **December 8, 2025** with a non-Chinese number are treated as "international" users under RedNote's Singapore entity ([WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/rednotes-great-divide-how-chinas-lifestyle-app-splits-worlds-to-chase-global-dreams/), page not readable, summary only). If that is true and it limits mainland distribution, a US-number account could fail for reasons that have nothing to do with the content. Check this before reading anything into the first results.

## WeChat Channels lacks the one asset it depends on

Every description of WeChat Channels says recommendation starts from the friend graph. Videos go first to the creator's circle, friends' likes surface them in the 朋友 (Friends) tab, and that engagement opens the next traffic tier ([Tencent Cloud](https://developer.cloud.tencent.com/article/2534874); [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1030623009_723902)). All of these are agency or training-school opinion. Tencent publishes no weighting. The one official mechanic found is linking a Channels account to a 公众号 (official account) with the same owner ([WeChat help center](https://findeross.weixin.qq.com/cgi-bin/mmfindernodelivecrmwebbroker-bin/helper-center/pages/GqqxqNGAOOT6FLby)). An overseas company posting AI-voice videos has no mainland friend network to seed, so on Channels it relies on the weaker interest feed, which no source measures for an account without one. Demographics point the same way. Channels has no official demographic data, and the third-party picture is unreliable: the most-cited report covers only AI-identified ecommerce buyers aged 40+ and calls its own estimates rough ([Youwang PDF](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202501141641912499_1.pdf)), while an agency says 40% of users are aged 26–30 ([Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1363193.html)). Xiaohongshu at least documents a large, urban, search-first parenting audience.

| | Xiaohongshu | WeChat Channels |
|---|---|---|
| How a new account gets seen | Interest feed plus search; officially 70%+ of users search | Friend graph first (agency consensus); interest feed second |
| Fit for 6.5–8 min 16:9 explainers | Horizontal default for mid-long video since May 2026; 90-day window | No sourced format signal |
| Documented parent audience | 88M parenting population; 37% tier-1 / new tier-1 | No official demographics |
| AI voice | Per-post declaration required; AI-hosted accounts banned | Same CAC rule; batch homogeneous posting banned ([36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3259734457430277)) |
| Links / app mention | Strictly banned everywhere, including DMs | Inside WeChat, 公众号 linkage is native |
| Long-term role | Discovery and brand search | Retention via 公众号 and groups, once there is an audience |

The two platforms do different jobs. Xiaohongshu is the better bet for strangers finding Vantee. Channels becomes useful later, once there are parents willing to forward videos into their 家长群 (parent group chats) and a 公众号 to bind. Do not read the four Channels posts as a verdict yet: they went up on November 8–9 and have essentially no data. Posting the same original video on both platforms is common practice. No source reports cross-platform duplicate penalties for a creator's own work. The monitored behaviors are 搬运 (reposting other people's content) and batches of near-identical posts ([woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6124879.html)). Strip any watermark, end card or handle that points to the other platform.

## A four-to-eight-week test plan

What is ready in the repo shapes the first move. There are 73 Chinese video folders, 72 of them with Chinese metadata. About 57 are AMC 8 and 13 are AMC 10, plus the two contest guides. Only **8 have a vertical `short.mp4` and a 3:4 `cover-3x4.jpg`**: the two guides, midpoint-chase (AMC 8), and five AMC 10 explainers (geometric-triangle, circles-through-grid-points, domino-tromino-2x6, lattice-triangle-10, polygon-center). That ready set leans toward AMC 10, the smaller market. Fix that before the test by rendering shorts and covers for three or four AMC 8 videos, using the existing `render.sh … short cn` and `Cover.tsx` path. Good candidates are topics Chinese parents already know as classic 奥数 (olympiad math) types: goats-and-grass (the 牛吃草 grazing problem), meet-twice (相遇问题, a meeting problem), dot-grid-squares and seven-point-star. That choice is my inference, not something tested.

| Priority | When | What | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before posting | Logged-in checks (list below); choose brand or persona account; render AMC 8 shorts and covers | Without the region and format checks, the results can't be interpreted |
| 2 | Weeks 1–4 | **3 full explainers a week** (16:9, native mid-long upload), in a roughly 2:1 AMC 8 : AMC 10 mix, starting with a problem explainer; the contest guides are the posts closest to "竞赛 promotion", so track their reach separately | Tick 「笔记含AI合成内容」 every time; post by hand; a 9–11 am China-time slot is anecdotally best ([woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/6303211.html)) |
| 3 | Weeks 1–4 | **2 vertical shorts a week**, each from a full explainer already posted | Separate note, separate title; hook ends in "你选哪个？" (which would you pick?) so the reply stays on-platform |
| 4 | Each post | Cover: 3:4 big-text cover (existing `cover-3x4.jpg`); test 2–4 covers if the desktop publisher still offers cover A/B ([woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/marketing/5572030.html)) | No answer on the cover, no logo URL |
| 5 | Each post | Title (about 20 characters, to confirm in app): keyword first, hook second, e.g. "AMC8几何同类题｜20个点能组成几个正方形？" Put "AMC8" in the title on half the posts and leave the current "数学思维讲解" style on the other half, to test search | Never "真题"; never 小升初/提分/保过 |
| 6 | Each post | Body: problem in one line, which solution methods are shown, one practice question; hashtags such as #AMC8 #AMC10 #数学思维 #奥数 #小学数学 #初中数学 #一题多解 (one problem, many solutions). These tags are **untested**; check their topic-page view counts in the app first | No vantee.ai, no download line, no "私信领" (DM to get) |
| 7 | Daily | Reply to comments as a person within the first hours | Signals a human-run account; comments sit next to the video now |
| 8 | Week 4 review | Per note: views, average watch time / completion, saves, save-to-like ratio, comments, follows from note; whether each note appears in search for its own keyword; AMC-titled vs non-AMC titles; full explainer vs short | Compare against the same videos on Channels |
| 9 | Week 8 decision | Continue if a meaningful share of notes earn saves above about half of likes and the account is steadily gaining followers from search; stop or rethink if views stay flat in the low hundreds after 20+ notes | These thresholds are judgment calls, not benchmarks; the 90-day window means late growth counts too |

Doubling the cadence or adding a 图文 carousel per video (problem, key figure, trap, "answer in the video") is the natural next step if saves are strong. Carousels are the platform's native knowledge format, but no source tested them against video for math. Do not batch-upload the backlog. Twenty near-identical posts in a week is the pattern AI-hosting enforcement targets.

**Check by hand while logged in to Xiaohongshu:**

1. Search the 视频 (video) tab for "AMC8讲解", "AMC8真题", "AMC10", "美国数学竞赛" and "袋鼠数学" (Math Kangaroo). Does anyone already post animated single-problem explainers? Write down the top accounts' follower counts.
2. For the top 20 notes under "AMC8", record likes, saves and comments, and whether each is video or 图文. This becomes your benchmark.
3. Check whether a new account on your phone number is treated as an "international" account, and whether a test note can be found by searching from a mainland account (ask a contact in China). This is the highest-impact unknown.
4. Check the maximum video length in the uploader, and confirm a 7–8 minute 16:9 file posts as horizontal mid-long video without cropping.
5. Check the title, body and hashtag limits, and the view counts for the topic pages you plan to tag.
6. Confirm the exact wording and location of the AI-content declaration toggle, and whether the profile offers an AI virtual-human tag.
7. Check whether the desktop publisher still allows multiple covers for A/B testing.
8. Check whether creator-incentive tasks (the traffic-coupon plan) show up for a new account.

## Conclusion

Thinking about Xiaohongshu as a vertical-video platform is a year out of date for this project. The platform is now actively buying the long, horizontal, save-worthy content Vantee already has in its backlog. And the channel's weaknesses on WeChat, no friend graph and an overseas operator, matter much less in a search-led feed. The real constraints on Xiaohongshu are behavioral, not about format. The account must look human-run (declared AI, hand-posted, varied, replying), must never point to the app, and must stay clear of admissions claims. Those constraints also mean Xiaohongshu will build awareness and brand searches, not direct installs. Judge it on saves and search visibility, not app downloads.

The open question is whether the empty search means an unclaimed niche or no demand. Nothing in the research settles it. A small test on 20–30 posts, with a decision at week eight, does: the empty niche costs nothing to claim, and the downside is a few hours of hand-posting already-rendered videos. Check the international-account question first. A good-looking channel that mainland parents never see would produce the same flat numbers as no demand at all.
