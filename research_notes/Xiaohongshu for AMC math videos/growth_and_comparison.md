## 1. Xiaohongshu distribution mechanics (CES, traffic pools, search, 收藏, format/orientation, length, frequency, covers, titles)

### Takeaway
The most important finding is official and recent. Since 2026-05-26, Xiaohongshu has made **横屏 (16:9) the default for 中长视频**. It now treats 收藏 / 关注 / 长时播放 / 横屏 / 弹幕 as core ranking signals and extends recommendation of good mid-long videos to 90 days. So 3–6 min 16:9 full solutions are not penalized; they are the format the platform is pushing. The widely quoted CES formula (like 1, save 1, comment 4, share 4, follow 8) is a third-party model with no official source. Search is a major discovery path: in 2024 H1, more than 70% of MAU used search, per an official report seen through a repost.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, 2026-05-26 RED 新生代创作盛典]** Xiaohongshu made horizontal the default for 中长视频, opened free 4K to all users, and showed comments on the same screen as the video. It made 收藏, 关注, 长时播放, 横屏 and 弹幕 core algorithm signals for mid-long video, and extended the recommendation window for mid-long video to 90 days. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103201938.html); [NBD 2026-05-27 (fetched: confirms 永夏 announced the horizontal default and comments on the same screen; the core-signal list including 横屏 and 弹幕, and the 90-day window, are reported but not attributed to a speaker)](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-05-27/4410421.html); [Tencent News 2026-05-27](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260527A09PBV00); [CLS](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2382224)
- **[OFFICIAL, same event]** Support on offer:
  - Mature creators: a fund of tens of millions of yuan a year, up to 1,000,000 yuan per project.
  - Small but promising creators: 1-on-1 operations support.
  - Video incentive plan: up to 80,000 traffic coupons per creator per month for completing update tasks, 50 billion (500亿) traffic in total.
  - 蒲公英 video revenue share: in testing.

- **[OFFICIAL via media, 2026-08]** Exposure share of good mid-long video rose 5x in six months, and mid-long video views grew more than 30% YoY. - **[ANECDOTE/OPINION, 2024-06-24]** The CES formula: 点赞×1 + 收藏×1 + 评论×4 + 转发×4 + 关注×8.
  - The article calls it "widely accepted" but cites no official source.
  - It describes a pipeline: review and tag matching, then an initial traffic pool, then promotion based on score.

  - Its advice: focus on the first image ("注重首图"), use pain-point titles, add "适当的矛盾" (some tension), and write lists, guides and reviews, which go viral more easily.

- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION]** One source adds a time-decay factor to CES. — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6201172.html)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION]** A creator's own test found that click-through rate matters more than raw interaction counts. — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/5913844.html)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION]** Search-results ranking depends on (1) how well the keywords match and (2) how much interaction the note gets shortly after posting. — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/5865982.html); [36Kr](https://36kr.com/p/2342185609795072) (not fetched)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2024 H1, seen via repost; older than 2025]** From the 《小红书十大搜索趋势洞察》 report: more than 70% of MAU use search, users search about 6 times a day, and one-third search first when they open the app. — [Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1359882.html)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION]** Personal posting-time data: posts at 9–11 am got the best exposure. — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/6303211.html)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION]** Text-only covers suit tutorial and knowledge bloggers, if the text itself is compelling. — [woshipm (cover design article)](https://www.woshipm.com/marketing/5572030.html)

### Inferences
- The 2026 official signal list (收藏 / 关注 / long watch time weighted up) overrides the folk CES formula for mid-long video, where saves were "worth 1". For a math explainer, **收藏 is likely the key action**: parents save solutions to show their kids. A worked solution parents want to keep fits these signals better than entertainment does.
- Full solutions (16:9, 3–6 min) can go up as native horizontal mid-long video, with no crop needed. The 30–40 s vertical teasers compete in a different, short-video feed. The horizontal finding says nothing about how teasers perform.
- The 90-day recommendation window plus search-led discovery favors evergreen, keyword-rich titles over trend timing. Examples: "AMC8 真题", "AMC10 2024 第X题", "几何 一题多解", "小升初/初中 数学竞赛".
- Enforcement against AI-hosted accounts means the AI-voice account should look clearly human-run. Concretely: a stated persona, replies to comments, and probably Xiaohongshu's AI-content label if offered. This is inference; no source says AI voice-over is penalized.

### Gaps
- No official CES weights or traffic-pool sizes. Third-party figures for the "initial pool" (e.g. 200–500 exposures) come from weak sources and are left out on purpose.
- No data on **图文 vs 视频 reach**.
- No data on the **ideal length for 知识/教育 video**, and no official minimum duration for 中长视频.
- No data on **posting frequency**.
- No tested **cover (3:4 big text)** or **title formula** data beyond "注重首图" and pain-point / tension titles.
- No official 2025–2026 figure for the share of users who search.

## 2. What works for 知识类/学习类 accounts on Xiaohongshu (carousels, 一题多解, series, comments, 真题 giveaways, case studies)

### Takeaway
No sourced case study of a math, 奥数 or AMC creator growing from zero was found. Generic cold-start advice applies (find benchmark accounts, review the data, keep each post useful). The platform's own push toward saves and watch time favors 题目+解析 content people want to keep. Tactics specific to education remain unverified.

- Comment bait ("你选哪个?") fits the 4x comment weight in the folk CES and the new comments-beside-video UI. The bait must stay on the platform: "评论区领真题PDF" plus a DM or WeChat handoff falls under 导流, which is monitored.

## 3. WeChat Channels mechanics (social graph, 公众号 linkage, 私域, education growth, reach without friends)

### Takeaway
Many sources say WeChat Channels recommendation is social-first: friends' likes push a video to the "朋友" tab, and friend engagement opens the next traffic tier. All of these sources are agency or training-institution opinion. The only official mechanic found is 公众号 binding. A new account with no WeChat friend network would lose the main advantage claimed for Channels, and no source measures how far such an account reaches.

### Cited Findings
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION, 2026-06-01, training institution 丝路赞学院]** Social recommendation "占据主导地位" (dominates) over interest recommendation. Videos liked by friends show in the 朋友 tab, where CTR is "several times" higher. The suggested funnel: short video → live → groups → 公众号 → mini-program. Paid traffic is said to be above 80% in some categories, with ROI down from above 3 to about 1.5. These figures are unsourced ("有研究显示"). — [Sohu (fetched)](https://www.sohu.com/a/1030623009_723902)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION, 2025]** Videos go first to the creator's friend circle. Friends' likes and shares decide whether the video reaches the next pool. Completion rate, like rate and the first 3 seconds matter. — [Tencent Cloud developer article](https://developer.cloud.tencent.com/article/2534874)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION, 2021; older]** In the early (cold-start) phase, social recommendation helps new creators get their first views. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/1301600754614920)
- **[OFFICIAL]** A Channels account can be bound to a 公众号 if they belong to the same entity (主体) or share the same admin WeChat ID. The binding can be changed within 2 hours, then not for 6 months. — [WeChat Channels help center](https://findeross.weixin.qq.com/cgi-bin/mmfindernodelivecrmwebbroker-bin/helper-center/pages/GqqxqNGAOOT6FLby)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION, older guides]**
  - Channels encourages original work; reposted (搬运) content gets no recommendation and may be penalized.
  - New videos get extra recommendation only after reaching some heat, so early views depend on forwards and shares.
  - Personal-interest verification needs sustained original content plus 100k+ followers on Channels or the 公众号.
  - Sources: [yunyingpai](https://www.yunyingpai.com/media/659331.html); [Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/648327943)
- **[OFFICIAL via 36Kr]** Channels prohibits batch-posting homogeneous content built from similar scenarios, scripts or elements. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3259734457430277)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2023 公开课; older]** Daily active creators grew more than 100% in 2022, and plays of original content rose 350% YoY. Tencent has never published detailed Channels DAU/MAU. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103006654.html); [ITHome](https://www.ithome.com/0/666/734.htm)
- **[ANECDOTE, unsourced]** Channels MAU of about 800M and 70 min/day of use. — [Tencent News 2025-06-17](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250617A03PCG00) (no source given; treat as unverified)

### Inferences
- With no friend network, Channels depends on the weaker interest-based feed. The practical levers on Channels:
  - Seed each post into parent WeChat groups (家长群) or Moments, but only where the audience consents. Spammy forwarding risks bans.
  - Bind a 公众号 for long-form text and SEO inside WeChat search.
  - Use groups or 企业微信 (private traffic, 私域) for retention.
- For the US-based creator, Channels growth probably comes after building an audience elsewhere, e.g. moving Xiaohongshu followers' parent groups onto WeChat. This is inference.

### Gaps
- No data on how far a no-network account reaches on Channels.
- No official breakdown of how the algorithm weights social vs interest signals.
- No case studies of education or math accounts on Channels in 2024–2026.

## 4. Demographics: Xiaohongshu vs WeChat Channels, and fit for parents of 10–15-year-olds

### Takeaway
On Xiaohongshu, 54.2% of users are under 30 (QuestMobile, Oct 2025), so about 46% are 30+, the parent age group. There is a large 母婴 base (88M active in 2023) and a historic ~70/30 female/male split, the male share having risen to about 30% by 2023 (older data). WeChat Channels has **no official demographic data**. Third-party reports stress users over 40, but they rest on AI-estimated, ecommerce-focused samples. For affluent, tier-1, education-focused parents (妈妈 especially) researching competitions, Xiaohongshu is the better-documented fit.

### Cited Findings
- **[3RD-PARTY DATA, QuestMobile, 2025-12-16]** As of Oct 2025:
  - Under 30: Xiaohongshu 54.2%, Weibo 58.6%, Bilibili 66.5%.
  - Xiaohongshu users in tier-1 and new-tier-1 cities: 37.2%.
  - Douyin users aged 51+: 22.2%.
  - The report has no WeChat Channels breakdown.
  - Source: [Digitaling/QuestMobile (fetched)](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1441257.html)

  - The definitions behind each figure differ and could not be reconciled.
  - Sources: [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4868082.html); [Xueqiu](https://xueqiu.com/8315851674/320853541/351728602); [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/6428459.html)

## 5. Repurposing across both platforms (adaptations; duplicate-content / 搬运 detection)

### Takeaway
Both platforms penalize **搬运** (reposting others' content) and Channels bans batch homogeneous content. **No source was found on cross-platform detection of the same creator posting the same original video on both.** Posting your own original video on both appears to be standard practice, but no source confirms it is safe. On Xiaohongshu, 16:9 full solutions can go up as-is. Vertical teasers suit both short feeds.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, XHS community rules]** 八大重点监测: 虚构体验, 夸大其词, 低质创作, 过度P图, **素材搬运**, 诱导行为, **导流行为**, 拉踩. Reposting another creator's content requires @-ing the original author. — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6124879.html); [Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/954169.html)
- **[OFFICIAL via 36Kr]** Channels bans batch-posting homogeneous content built from similar scenarios or scripts. Douyin counts the 原创识别率 (originality recognition rate) as a core metric and rates highly repetitive content as low quality. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3259734457430277)
- **[ANECDOTE/OPINION]** A single note with unusually low views usually hit a banned word or used a non-compliant cover. Check for throttling by searching for your account or note. — [Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1066743.html); [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6350308.html)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2026-05]** Xiaohongshu: horizontal is the default for mid-long video. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103201938.html)

### Inferences
- Adaptations for each platform:
  - Remove watermarks, end cards and handles that point to the other platform. Both platforms monitor sending users elsewhere; Xiaohongshu treats it as 导流 explicitly.
  - Write titles and body text for each platform: keyword-rich for Xiaohongshu search; for Channels, a forward-friendly hook ("转给孩子做做看").
  - Make a 3:4 big-text cover for Xiaohongshu image notes and the vertical teaser.
  - Burn in Chinese captions on both.
  - The 16:9 teaser problem applies only to the vertical cut.
- Batch-posting near-identical teasers on Channels could trip the homogeneity rule. Vary the hooks and scripts between teasers.

### Gaps
- No platform rule or tested report on whether the same original video posted on Xiaohongshu and Channels gets throttled on either.
- No evidence on how either platform's watermark or fingerprint detection treats self-uploaded cross-posts.
