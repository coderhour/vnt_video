# AMC 8 / AMC 10 content landscape on Xiaohongshu (小红书 / RED), 2025–2026

Method note (read first): Xiaohongshu search and note pages could not be read directly. `xiaohongshu.com/search_result?keyword=AMC8真题讲解` returned only page chrome (tabs 全部/图文/视频/用户, no notes), and `/mobile/question/...` pages redirect to `rednote.com/explore`. Search-engine indexing of Xiaohongshu is thin and mostly returns unrelated "question" aggregator pages. So **no like, 收藏 or comment counts, follower counts or per-account data could be collected**. Everything about formats on the platform below comes from a few indexed snippets plus indirect evidence (training-institution sites, data-platform articles). Inferences are labeled as such. Most market-size numbers come from training institutions selling AMC courses, which have an incentive to inflate them.

## Is there an active AMC 8/10 audience in China / on Xiaohongshu, who is it, and how big is AMC interest?

### Takeaway
AMC 8 is a large, growing mass-market contest in China: about 80,000 Chinese participants in 2025, with institution claims of 80–90k+ for 2026. It now skews to grades 4–6 because families use it as a "hard signal" for 小升初 and international schools, even though Beijing's 2025 rules formally ban using contest certificates in compulsory-education admissions. The audience is parents. The searches found no direct Xiaohongshu audience data for AMC.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 China participation:** global AMC 8 participation passed 300,000 in 2025; the China region had close to 80,000 (近8万). Source: AMC China club site (an AMC training/registration site, not MAA), article dated 2026-07-17 — [amcclub.org.cn](https://amcclub.org.cn/news/4446/)
- **2026 China participation (claims disagree):**
  - "Domestic registrations passed 80,000" (国内报考人数已经突破8万), article dated 2026-08-30 — [jingsailian.com](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html)
  - Other institution pages, per a search-tool summary: 2026 China 90k+ / global 320k+; an older forecast of China passing 100k for 2026; a claimed 20–25% annual growth rate. Which page says which was not confirmed; candidates are [犀牛 x-newedu.com](https://www.x-newedu.com/n/202607/64668.html) and [犀牛 mengma-edu.com](https://m.mengma-edu.com/n/202501/4673.html)
  - None of these cite MAA data.
- **Beijing alone:** about 7,500 students took the 2025 AMC 8. The same article says AMC 8 grew from "tens of thousands" a year to about 100k entries (人次) — [Huxiu, 2026-05-29](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4862663.html) (confirmed by fetching the page; the article gives no year for the 100k figure and does not mention 小升初 or 小红书).
- **2024 global numbers (older):** about 272,359 participants, about 231,478 of them in the US. No China figure was given. Search-tool summary; source URL not confirmed (possibly [liuxue.xdf.cn](https://liuxue.xdf.cn/blog/blog_7725421.shtml)).
- **Separate China papers in 2026:** China ran three papers: the US original, an "Astan" (阿斯丹) version and a China Organizing Committee version, each with its own cutoffs.
  - Cutoffs: top 1% at 24 / 24 / 22, top 5% at 21 / 21 / 18, top 25% at 16 / 16 / 14 — [amcclub.org.cn](https://amcclub.org.cn/news/4446/)
  - This suggests paper-security concerns. A change.org petition to MAA says the AMC has repeatedly suffered test leakage — [change.org](https://www.change.org/p/constructive-suggestions-for-strengthening-the-integrity-and-fairness-of-amc-competitions)
- **Getting younger:**
  - Grade 4–6 students rose from about 7% of entrants (2015) to about 65% (2025), per a search-tool summary of institution articles. Source URL not confirmed; possibly one of the 犀牛 pages above.
  - Another unidentified source puts the primary-school share at only about 26% (2024 data). The figures conflict — treat both as unreliable.
  - The share of 4th/5th graders is said to be rising every year — [jingsailian.com](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html)
- **Admissions role / policy:**
  - Beijing's 2025 compulsory-education admissions guidance: "义务教育入学严禁以考试成绩和各类竞赛证书、培训竞赛成绩、考级证明等作为招生依据" and "严禁以面试、评测、接收简历等形式选拔学生" — [Xinhua, 2025-04-18](https://www.news.cn/local/20250418/287fb0ec4f634bd6b6770c4685f4c678/c.html)
  - Even so, institutions still market AMC 8 as "小升初的必须项" (a must-have for 小升初) — [犀牛 x-newedu.com](https://www.x-newedu.com/n/202607/64613.html)
  - In Shanghai parent circles, AMC 8 is rumored to be "三公标配" (standard for the top 3 private middle schools), per a Phoenix/ifeng piece. Search-tool summary; which of the two ifeng URLs ([1](https://news.ifeng.com/c/8i5zb5ErGSL), [2](https://news.ifeng.com/c/8eQVogc0oaI)) says this was not confirmed.
  - The Ministry of Education uses a whitelist: only 44 national K-12 competitions were sanctioned in 2022, each at most once a year — [Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1014463).
- **No crackdown found:** I found no evidence of a 2024–2025 cancellation or suspension of AMC 8 in mainland China. Searches in English and Chinese turned up nothing. The 2026 contest ran in January 2026 with separate China papers (sources above).
- **Xiaohongshu has a large parenting audience** (indirect evidence):
  - Qiangua (千瓜) / Xiaohongshu reports describe mother-and-baby (母婴) and parent groups that are still growing — [digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1384067.html)
  - A 2026 survey (n=2303) found parents there trust hands-on tests and real feedback more than authority — [digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1546502.html)
  - Newrank (新榜) keeps a daily Xiaohongshu 教育 (education) ranking — [newrank](https://www.newrank.cn/ranklist/xiaohongshu/10/1/2025-07-27). It was not fetched, and the search snippet showed no AMC-related accounts in it.
  - An Xiaohongshu education-industry marketing deck (2025 618/summer) calls the platform "3亿用户的生活指南" (a life guide for 300 million users) and tells education advertisers to plant search keywords at least 30 days ahead — [fxbaogao](https://www.fxbaogao.com/detail/5556939)

### Inferences
- AMC, a foreign contest, is presumably not on the Ministry of Education whitelist, which would explain why it lives in a grey "hard signal" zone. Not verified against the list itself.
- The likely Xiaohongshu AMC audience is mainland parents (mostly mothers) of grade 3–7 children in tier-1 cities and international or bilingual schools, plus some overseas Chinese parents. One indexed profile was an Australian education agency posting "AMC放榜" (results released). This matches the platform's parenting demographics and the shift of AMC 8 to younger kids, but it was not measured directly.
- About 80k+ yearly China entrants, each with at least one engaged parent, gives a niche of meaningful size. Interest is seasonal: registration in autumn, the contest in late January, results in spring, and "summer planning" content in July–August, which is when the institution articles above cluster.
- Admission bans push AMC talk into informal, parent-to-parent channels, which is what Xiaohongshu is for. Explicit "AMC for 小升初" claims may be sensitive. Framing videos as "思维训练" (thinking training) or fun problem-solving is safer.

### Gaps
- No official MAA China participation numbers. The ~80k figure comes from institution sites.
- No direct numbers on the size of the Xiaohongshu AMC audience (search volume, topic page views, follower counts). The 千瓜/新榜/蝉妈妈 data needs paid access.
- Not confirmed: whether Shanghai's 2025 rules explicitly mention contest certificates.
- The 2024 AMC 8 China cancellation / online-format issue named in the brief was searched only with generic policy and suspension terms (取消 / 违规 / 白名单; "suspended mainland test centers MAA"). Both searches came back empty. A targeted check, e.g. "AMC8 2024 中国 阿斯丹 线上 考试", was not done, so "no crackdown found" is weak evidence. The 2026 US-original / 阿斯丹 / 组委会 paper split hints at a story about who administers the contest.
- No AMC 10–specific China numbers were found. AMC 10 is likely much smaller than AMC 8.

## What kinds of AMC posts exist, who dominates, and is single-problem video explanation common?

### Takeaway
The indexed AMC content on Xiaohongshu is image-and-text (图文) notes: question-bank and resource promos, prep plans, and score and results posts. Most comes from training institutions (犀牛 / Rhino in particular), plus a few teachers and agencies. Single-problem video explainers exist in Mandarin on Douyin/Baidu-video-type channels and on Bilibili. On Xiaohongshu, I found no evidence of a polished, animated single-problem explainer series.

### Cited Findings
- **Indexed Xiaohongshu AMC snippets:**
  - A 犀牛竞赛 (Rhino) promo: "市面上只能找到一套套往年真题卷" (on the market you can only find sets of past papers); its teachers split AMC problems into 4 classes (algebra, combinatorics, geometry, number theory), 300 problems for AMC 8/10/12. This is a resource/lead-gen note.
  - A teacher's bilingual note works one classic number-theory problem (the light-switch problem) and ends with an AMC 8 真题 (past problem) as a challenge. This is a single-problem post, apparently 图文.
  - A note says AMC 8 tests thinking and solving methods, and long word problems need module-by-module training.
  - An Australian agency profile (Qs School GROUP) posts "AMC放榜" (results release) and 奥数思维 (olympiad thinking).
  - Source for all four: Xiaohongshu pages surfaced via search, e.g. [xiaohongshu.com/mobile/question/2837913](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/mobile/question/2837913), [Qs School GROUP profile](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/user/profile/61b83ae4000000001000d928). The underlying note URLs were not exposed and their content was paraphrased by the search tool.
- **Dominant institutions, and how they sell:**
  - 翰林 (Hanlin), 犀牛 (Rhino) and 唯寻 (Weixun) are the main AMC training brands.
  - Hanlin's own comparison article says Hanlin started in 2017 and Rhino/Weixun after 2020.
  - Claimed per-lesson prices: about ¥300 (Hanlin), ¥450 (Rhino), ¥500 (Weixun). This is biased, self-promotional material — [hanlin.com](https://www.hanlin.com/archives/1207350), [hanlin.com](https://www.hanlin.com/archives/1207397)
  - Their web articles (Rhino x-newedu / ix-edu / mengma-edu, jingsailian) follow one template: contest news, then a cutoff analysis, then a prep plan, then a WeChat ID for contact (e.g. a WeChat contact ID in jingsailian promo blocks) — [jingsailian.com](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html)
  - The cutoff-analysis step relies on tactics like "2027年备考AMC8不能再走老路了" ("prep for the 2027 AMC 8 can't follow the old path") and "试题难人数多" ("harder problems, more people").
  - Think Academy (学而思 overseas) posts prep "打卡" (daily check-in) plans and diagnostic tests — [thethinkacademy.com](https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blog/think-academy-2026-amc8-cn/)
- **Single-problem videos on other Chinese platforms:**
  - Baidu video search shows many short videos titled like "某年AMC8真题第X题" ("AMC 8 [year] problem X") from accounts such as "小学数学amc8竞赛", e.g. "2004年amc8第20题". The hosting platform was not identified; probably Douyin or Haokan — [Baidu vsearch](https://m.baidu.com/vsearch/xsppage?pd=video_page&atn=vertical&dsp=iphone&resource_id=5021&from_sf=1&word=amc8%E5%8E%86%E5%B9%B4%E7%9C%9F%E9%A2%98%E5%85%8D%E8%B4%B9) (via search-tool summary)
  - Bilibili hosts lecture-style series: Po-Shen Loh's "全美数学竞赛（AMC）8年级入门精讲", a series of 25 videos — [bilibili BV1T3411w7zG](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1T3411w7zG/)
  - Bilibili also has paid bilingual AMC 10 真题精讲 courses that funnel to a website — [bilibili cheese](https://www.bilibili.com/cheese/play/ss880362984)
  - Po-Shen Loh's LIVE site has Chinese pages for individual problems, e.g. 2025 AMC 8 problem 7 — [live.poshenloh.com](https://live.poshenloh.com/zh/past-contests/amc8/2025/problem/7)
- **Text resources:** CSDN carries serialized text write-ups of past papers, often with "免费送题" (free problems) hooks — [CSDN](https://blog.csdn.net/xiegangfou/article/details/137703885)

### Inferences
- Single-problem Mandarin AMC videos exist in China, but the typical form is a teacher recording a whiteboard or screen (Douyin/Baidu) or a long lecture (Bilibili). I found no sign of animated, produced explainers with full multi-solution and trap coverage on Xiaohongshu. "Animated AI-voice single-problem explainer" therefore looks like a real format gap on Xiaohongshu, but not a gap in Chinese video overall.
- On Xiaohongshu, institutions use AMC notes mainly as lead generation: resource packs, plans and cutoff news drive "私信/评论领取" (DM or comment to receive) and then a move to WeChat. A finished, give-it-all-away solution video doesn't fit that funnel, which plausibly explains why institutions don't make it.

### Gaps
- Could not list the top Xiaohongshu AMC accounts or their follower counts. Searches for 犀牛/翰林/唯寻 Xiaohongshu accounts returned nothing.
- Could not see the 视频 (video) tab of the AMC8 search results, so the video vs 图文 split on Xiaohongshu is unknown.
- No information on AMC live streams on Xiaohongshu.

## Engagement signals: likes / 收藏 / comments; which topics earn the most 收藏?

### Takeaway
No engagement data could be retrieved; this is the biggest gap in these notes. Indirect evidence suggests that 收藏-heavy topics are resource lists, 真题 packs, cutoff analysis and timelines.

### Cited Findings
- The topics institutions repeat most often are:
  - categorized 真题 packs ("300道分类练习题", 300 categorized practice problems)
  - "吃透2000-2024年600道真题（免费送题）" (master 600 past problems from 2000–2024, free problems included)
  - cutoff analysis
  - staged prep plans (foundation 6–8 months out, advanced 3–4 months, sprint 1–2 months with 2–3 timed mocks a week)
  - Sources: [CSDN](https://blog.csdn.net/xiegangfou/article/details/137703885), [jingsailian.com](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html), [amcclub.org.cn](https://amcclub.org.cn/news/4446/)
- Xiaohongshu's education ad guidance stresses search: plant keywords early for search-recommendation traffic, and expect education ad prices to fluctuate in 2025 — [fxbaogao](https://www.fxbaogao.com/detail/5556939)

### Inferences
- Based on general Xiaohongshu behavior, not measured for AMC: 收藏 (saves) is the key metric for utility content, so "真题 + 解析" carousels, plans and resource lists probably earn many saves. Comments are probably driven by "求资料" (asking for the materials).
- A video explainer could borrow this by pairing each video with a 图文 carousel (the problem, the key figure, the trap) and titling it for search, e.g. "AMC8 2024 第X题 | 90%的人掉进这个坑" ("AMC 8 2024 problem X | 90% of people fall into this trap"), and putting the full solution in the video.

### Gaps
- No typical like/收藏/comment numbers for AMC notes or videos. These would need a logged-in manual sample (e.g. the top 20 notes for "AMC8", "AMC8真题", "AMC10") or paid 千瓜/新榜 queries.

## Is "I didn't find similar videos on Xiaohongshu" consistent with the evidence?

### Takeaway
Yes, broadly. Every piece of indexed evidence points to AMC on Xiaohongshu being image-and-text, institution-driven and focused on lead generation. Single-problem video solutions in Mandarin live mostly on Douyin/Bilibili, as plain teacher recordings. A produced, animated single-problem explainer on Xiaohongshu appears to be rare or missing. This could not be verified exhaustively without logging in.

### Cited Findings
- The Xiaohongshu AMC snippets that were indexed are 图文 promos, resource packs and single 图文 problems ([xiaohongshu.com](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/mobile/question/2837913)). Video explainers turn up instead on Bilibili ([bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1T3411w7zG/)) and on Baidu-indexed short-video accounts ([Baidu vsearch](https://m.baidu.com/vsearch/xsppage?pd=video_page&atn=vertical&dsp=iphone&resource_id=5021&from_sf=1&word=amc8%E5%8E%86%E5%B9%B4%E7%9C%9F%E9%A2%98%E5%85%8D%E8%B4%B9)).
- Institutions' content is built to capture contacts (WeChat IDs, free-material hooks) — [jingsailian.com](https://www.jingsailian.com/like/1649013.html), [CSDN](https://blog.csdn.net/xiegangfou/article/details/137703885)

### Inferences
- Likely reasons for the gap:
  1. Xiaohongshu culture is image-first and save-driven, so carousels cost less to make and earn many saves.
  2. Institutions want DMs and WeChat adds, not complete free solutions.
  3. Animated explainers are expensive for individual teachers.
  4. Xiaohongshu throttles links and off-platform redirects (consistent with the project's existing "no link on screen" rule), so explainer creators are pushed toward Douyin/Bilibili.
- **Opportunity:** a consistent, animated, trap-focused explainer series would stand out. 16:9 full solutions are a weaker fit for Xiaohongshu's vertical feed than the 30–40 s vertical teasers. The likely play is vertical teasers on Xiaohongshu, with full 16:9 videos on WeChat Channels and Bilibili, plus 图文 problem carousels for 收藏.
- **Risks:**
  - AI-voice content must be declared.
  - Tying content explicitly to admissions (小升初) collides with Beijing's 2025 rules.
  - Problem copyright: MAA owns the AMC problems. Using original or adapted problems, as the Vantee quiz bank does, avoids this.

### Gaps
- Not checked by hand: the Xiaohongshu 视频 tab for "AMC8 讲解", "AMC8真题", "美国数学竞赛" and "袋鼠数学". A 10-minute logged-in check would confirm or refute the gap.
- No data on whether 袋鼠数学 (Math Kangaroo) or 数学思维 animated content (aimed at younger kids) already fills a similar niche.
