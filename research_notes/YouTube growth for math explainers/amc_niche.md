# The AMC 8 / AMC 10 / MATHCOUNTS explainer niche on YouTube (2025–2026)

Research note: see the Gaps sections for limitations. YouTube watch pages and several key sites (maa.org, AoPS Wiki problem pages, a Singapore news site) returned 403 or no stats to the fetch tool, so **per-video view counts could not be verified**. Most competitor and "best channel" sources are prep companies recommending themselves or each other, not independent rankings. Treat every subscriber number as third-party and approximate.

## 1. Established channels, formats and what top videos share

### Takeaway
The competition-math niche has two layers. The first is a few large general "math puzzle" channels with millions of subscribers, built on single-problem and visual-intuition videos (3Blue1Brown, Mind Your Decisions). The second is a long tail of AMC-specific channels: AoPS, OmegaLearn/Sohil Rathi, AlphaStar, TMAS, LIVE by Po-Shen Loh, Michael Penn, MathTopia Academy, Daily Dose of Math, STEM Simplified, FormulaFocus, SpreadTheMathLove, Thinking Feet, MadMath and others. These make past-contest walkthroughs, and a lot of their discovery comes from links on AoPS Wiki problem pages. Even AoPS, the category authority, has only about 113K subscribers and posts rarely. That points to a small, high-intent niche with no dominant video brand.

Founder Richard Rusczyk appears on camera — [AoPS YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/ArtofProblemSolving); [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/artofproblemsolving/)

The page's methodology is undisclosed and partly AI-written — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/artofproblemsolving/)
- **Example of the AoPS format:** "Art of Problem Solving: 2019 AMC 10 A #25 / AMC 12 A #24", a single hard problem cited by contest, year and number — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9klaWnZojq0)

### Inferences
- Almost every AMC-specific video is titled **"<year> AMC <8|10A|10B> Problem <n>"** or **"<year> AMC … Solutions"**, because viewers arrive by searching an exact problem, often from an AoPS Wiki link. These videos are evergreen but capped by the number of people who took that paper.
- The big general channels (MYD, 3B1B) win on one striking problem plus a visual "aha", not on contest coverage. Vantee's format (one hard problem, animated diagrams, traps, a variant) sits between the two layers: closer to MYD in shape, aimed at AMC students.
- The AMC-specific field is fragmented, and many channels are student-run or tutoring-company channels. If, as assumed but not verified, most use whiteboard or screen-recorded production, animation quality and a consistent character are realistic differentiators.

### Gaps

- "Math with Mr. J" was not researched. From general knowledge (unverified here) it targets elementary or middle-school basics, not contests.
- No data on which AMC videos are the top performers by views.

## 2. Seasonality, contest dates, search terms and copyright

### Takeaway
There are two hard annual peaks. AMC 10/12 runs in early to mid November (A and B about a week apart). AMC 8 runs in late January, in a testing window, not on a single day. Searches cluster around "<year> AMC <test> <A/B> Problem <n>", "solutions", "answer key" and "cutoff", spiking in the days after each sitting. Then a prep wave builds in the 1–3 months before each test. Official problems belong to the MAA, but channels routinely solve them citing year and problem number. The AoPS Wiki hosts the problem text and links those videos. No explicit MAA video policy was found.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 AMC 10A/12A:** November 5, 2025 (MAA 2025–26 page, via search snippet) — [MAA](https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/). AMC 10B/12B was reported by third parties as November 13, 2025 — [Think Academy](https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blogamc-10-faq-amp-resources-your-ultimate-guide)
- **2026 AMC 10/12 (mainland China, ASDAN):** registration deadline October 26, 2026. Paper A is Thursday, November 5, 2026, and Paper B is Friday, November 13, 2026, each 75 minutes. AMC 10/12 are at official offline test centers only; "at-home online testing is no longer supported" — [AMC Club (ASDAN partner)](https://en.amcclub.org.cn/blogs/571.html)
- **2026 AMC 8:** testing window January 22–30, 2026, per Areteem citing MAA updates — [Areteem](https://programs.areteem.org/?p=2677). Local sittings included January 24, 2026 (AoPS Academy Redmond) — [AoPS Redmond](https://sites.google.com/artofproblemsolving.com/rm-contest/home/amc-8-1012)
- **2027 AMC 8:** January 22, 2027, with China registration closing January 12, 2027 — [AMC Club](https://en.amcclub.org.cn/blogs/571.html); [ASDAN](https://www.seedasdan.asia/en/amc8-en/)
- **2026 format:** the 2026 AMC is in person only — [AMC Club](https://en.amcclub.org.cn/blogs/1438.html)
- **Leak risk:** the staggered multi-day AMC 8 window has raised leak concerns. 2026 AMC 8 questions and answers were reportedly sold online in China on the first day of the Singapore sittings — [The Online Citizen, Jan 26, 2026](https://theonlinecitizen.com/2026/01/26/amc-8-test-questions-and-answers-leaked-online-on-first-day-of-singapore-sittings) (page 403; details from search summary)
- **Search phrasing visible in content titles:** "2026 AMC 8 Problems 1–15 Solutions", "All problems and solutions to the 2025 AMC 10A", "2026 AMC 10A Problems & Solutions: Answer Key + PDF Download", "2025 AMC 10A Answer Key", "AMC 8 2026 Answer Key & Score Cutoffs" — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tAGuV-p5O3I); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Svl1KYiW7Ts); [Think Academy](https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blog/2026-amc-10a-problems-solutions/); [Po-Shen Loh](https://live.poshenloh.com/past-contests/amc10/2025A/answers); [WuKong](https://www.wukongsch.com/blog/2026-amc-8-answer-key-post-61141/)
- **Prep-intent phrasing:** "How to prepare for AMC 8", "How to Prepare for AMC 10 Math Competition", "Best AMC Prep Courses of 2026" — [YouTube](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=atSMf1eegmU); [Think Academy](https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blog/how-to-prepare-for-amc-10-math-competition/); [Achievable](https://achievable.me/exams/amc-8-10-12/best-courses/)
- **Copyright:** no MAA statement on video use of problems was found. Under general YouTube rules, rights holders can file DMCA takedowns, and three strikes in 90 days terminates a channel — [Wikipedia: YouTube copyright issues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube_copyright_issues); [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6364458?hl=en-GB). Common practice is visible in titles: channels cite "<year> AMC 10 A #25" and solve the problem on screen, AoPS included — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9klaWnZojq0)

### Inferences
- **Calendar for Vantee:** an AMC 10 prep push from about mid September to November 12, and an AMC 8 prep push from November to about January 21. The days right after each sitting are when "<year> AMC … solutions" searches peak. That traffic mostly goes to real-problem walkthroughs, which Vantee does not make. Vantee could ride it with "AMC 10-style problem like 2026 10A #__"-type framing, or a "post-AMC: what to practice for AIME / next year" video. Today (2026-11-09) falls between 10A (Nov 5) and 10B (Nov 13), the peak AMC 10 week.
- Original AMC-style questions avoid MAA copyright exposure entirely, but they lose the exact-match "year + problem number" search traffic that drives the niche. Titles should therefore target topic and intent searches instead ("AMC 10 geometry trick", "hardest AMC 8 counting problem type", "AMC 8 practice problem").
- The AMC 8 "window" plus leaks means international viewers may search problems before US students finish. Avoid anything that resembles a leaked or current paper.
- A search-summary claim that "AMC 8 is usually run in the fall" is outdated: it appears to have moved to January in recent years (2026 window Jan 22–30; 2027 on Jan 22).

### Gaps
- No Google Trends curves were retrievable, and "AMC" is badly confounded by AMC Entertainment (stock and theaters) in search tools. Pull Trends by hand using the topic "American Mathematics Competitions" or queries like "AMC 8 practice" and "AMC 10 problems", US vs worldwide, over 5 years.
- The official MAA 2026 AMC 8 national date range and the 2025 10B date could not be fetched (maa.org returned 403).
- No MAA policy text on reproducing problems in videos was found. The safe assumption is that the problems are copyrighted, and that practice relies on tolerance or fair use.

## 3. Which content types get the most views

### Takeaway
Hard data is missing. Structurally, the niche produces three formats. Full or half-exam walkthroughs are long (Penn's is 1 h 42 m) and are reference material. Single-problem "Problem 25" or "Final Five" videos are the hard ones people search for. Prep strategy or roadmap guides attract parents and beginners. Score-cutoff and AIME-qualification content gets heavy text-blog coverage, which suggests strong search demand, but it is mostly served by blogs, not video.

### Cited Findings
- **Hardest-problems format:** AoPS curates the hardest problems as "AMC 10 Final Fives" — [AoPS YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/ArtofProblemSolving)
- **Full-exam walkthroughs:** examples include Michael Penn's 2025 AMC 10A (1 h 42 m) and the 2026 AMC 8 split videos — [Class Central](https://www.classcentral.com/course/youtube-all-problems-and-solutions-to-the-2025-amc-10a-504207); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VJ_E35On4ec)
- **Roadmap and strategy videos:** OmegaLearn's AMC 10/12→AIME→olympiad roadmap; "How to prepare for AMC 8 – Ultimate Study Guide" — [OmegaLearn](https://www.omegalearn.org/amc10); [YouTube](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=atSMf1eegmU)
- **2025 cutoffs (third-party):** AMC 10A AIME cutoff 105 (Distinction 112.5, Honor Roll 136.5); AMC 10B 99 (105, 133.5); average about 57. Cutoffs have been rising since 2020 — [Think Academy](https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blog/?p=16269). AMC 12A 96 and 12B 100.5 — [Areteem](https://areteem.org/blog/aime-cutoffs-and-amc-10-12-awards/); [Random Math](https://randommath.com/blogs/2025-26-aime-qualiications). Historical results — [AoPS Wiki](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/AMC_historical_results)
- **Who writes about cutoffs:** many tutoring firms publish cutoff, answer-key and "how to qualify for AIME" posts (Think Academy, Areteem, WuKong, Pengi) — [Think Academy case studies](https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blog/?p=21068); [Pengi](https://tutor.pengi.ai/blog/2025-amc-10-amc-12-cutoff-scores-qualification)
- **Big general channels:** Mind Your Decisions built about 3M+ subscribers on single puzzles and brain teasers — [SponsorRadar](https://sponsorradar.com/channels/mindyourdecisions)

### Inferences
- A single hard problem plus a "trick" or "trap" framing (the MYD-style hook) is the format with the most reach outside the contest-taker pool. It is also the format that suits original problems. Full-test walkthroughs need real papers, so Vantee should skip them.
- Cutoff and "Can I make AIME?" content is a timely, parent-heavy search that video barely serves. A short, accurate "what score do you need on AMC 10 for AIME (2025 cutoffs)" video is a low-cost gap. Wait for official MAA numbers before stating cutoffs.

### Gaps
- No view-count comparison across formats was available. The ranking above is structural reasoning, not measured data.

## 4. Who watches and what that means for packaging

### Takeaway
Sourced: AoPS's channel audience is reported as male-skewed and 18–34, with top countries India, US and UK. China alone has about 50,000 AMC 8 takers a year, and parent-facing content (dates, cutoffs, prep tips) is mostly written by tutoring firms. Inferred (unsourced): students (about 11–16) search exact problems and topics, parents search dates, cutoffs and "how to prepare", and the 18–34 skew probably reflects adults and coaches plus kids watching on parents' accounts.

### Cited Findings
- **AoPS audience (CreatorDB):** male-skewed with an 18–34 core; top countries India, US, UK. This is AoPS's whole channel, including parenting-podcast content like "Raising Problem Solvers" — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/artofproblemsolving/)
- **Parent-directed content exists on the leading channel:** "Invite Your Kids to Problem-Solve Alongside You: Raising Problem Solvers Podcast" and "AoPS Webinar: Unlocking the Joy of Math Competitions" — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/artofproblemsolving/)
- **Tutoring blogs aimed at parents:** much of the parent-oriented content (dates, registration, cutoffs, prep tips) comes from Asia-focused tutoring firms (Think Academy, WuKong, ASDAN partners) — [WuKong](https://www.wukongsch.com/blog/amc-8-2024-latest-guide-post-20745/); [Think Academy](https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blog/all-about-2025-amc-10-your-ultimate-guide-to-top-faqs/)
- **China:** about 50,000 candidates sit the AMC 8 each year (Zaobao, via a Singapore report) — [The Online Citizen](https://theonlinecitizen.com/2026/01/26/amc-8-test-questions-and-answers-leaked-online-on-first-day-of-singapore-sittings). ASDAN is the official mainland organizer for AMC 8/10/12 and AIME. China has its own score cutoffs — [ASDAN](https://www.seedasdan.asia/en/amc8-en/); [WuKong](https://www.wukongsch.com/blog/2026-amc-8-answer-key-post-61141/)
- **Eligibility:** AMC 8 requires grade 8 or below and age under 15.5 (ASDAN / WuKong). Wikipedia says 14.5, a conflict to check against MAA — [ASDAN](https://www.seedasdan.asia/en/amc8-en/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematics_Competitions)
- **AMC 8 vs AMC 10 practice:** AoPS says AMC 10 practice is no substitute for AMC 8 practice, because the topics differ — [AoPS Wiki AMC 8](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/AMC_8)

### Inferences
- **For students:** titles should name the contest and topic ("AMC 10 geometry: the incircle trick"), thumbnails should show a striking figure plus a short trap line, and a 6–12 minute single-problem length fits. Shorts serve as hooks.
- **For parents:** separate videos or playlists on "how to prepare for AMC 8 in 8 weeks", "AMC 10 cutoffs explained" and "AMC 8 vs MATHCOUNTS". Plain titles, a face or character, no LaTeX in thumbnails. This audience is also Vantee's app-buying audience.
- Keep AMC 8 and AMC 10 content clearly labeled and separated (playlists already do this), since audiences and topics differ.
- The Chinese audience is large and parent-driven, but YouTube is blocked in mainland China. The existing Xiaohongshu / WeChat Channels cut is the right channel for it, as CLAUDE.md already plans.

### Gaps
- No direct data on the split between student and parent viewers for AMC videos, or on age demographics (YouTube hides under-18 data).
- No data on MATHCOUNTS-specific YouTube demand, which this research did not reach.

## 5. Opportunities: gaps, collaborations and communities

### Takeaway
Gaps include high-production animated single-problem explainers for AMC 8/10 (assumed, not verified: no competitor videos could be opened, so production quality must be checked by hand before building positioning on it), parent-facing explainers (cutoffs, timelines, prep plans), and topic-organized practice (vs exam-organized). The highest-intent discovery surface is the AoPS ecosystem (Wiki "Video Solution" links, forums), but it is organized around official problems. Original-problem videos have no natural slot there.

### Cited Findings
- **AoPS Wiki discovery:** wiki problem pages list community "Video Solution by …" links from many small channels, making the wiki a de facto video directory — [AoPS Wiki 2025 10A P25](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/2025_AMC_10A_Problems/Problem_25)
- **Resource lists:** the AoPS Wiki AMC pages list YouTube resources (OmegaLearn, TMAS, Po-Shen Loh) — [AoPS Wiki AMC 10](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/AMC_10); [AoPS Wiki AMC 12](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/AMC_12)
- **Third-party "AMC resources" roundups:** these list YouTube channels (Michael Penn, Numberphile, 3B1B, Khan Academy), so placement in such lists is a discovery route — [AMC 10 resources (amc10.org.cn)](https://en.amc10.org.cn/news/245.html)
- **Student creators:** student-run channels like OmegaLearn are established players and possible collaborators — [OmegaLearn](https://omegalearn.org/)
- **Newsletter and teacher channel:** Wes Carroll's Substack covers AMC for a teacher and parent audience — [wescarroll.substack.com](https://wescarroll.substack.com/p/amc)
- **Reddit:** searches for AMC YouTube recommendations on Reddit returned only generic results (AMC theaters, general channel lists), with no usable thread data — [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/youtube-channels)

### Inferences
- **Gap: the "answer key" moment without the answer key.** Right after each sitting (now, Nov 5–13), publish "if you missed this type on the AMC 10, practice this" topic videos, plus a cutoffs explainer once MAA releases scores.
- **Gap: topic playlists ("AMC 10 counting traps", "AMC 8 area tricks").** These match how coaches assign practice. Problem-number videos can't be reorganized this way.
- **Collaboration targets:** student-run channels (OmegaLearn), local math circles and AoPS Academy-type centers (which host sittings), and Chinese and Asian tutoring firms that write parent blogs. An animated tutor character is easy to license or embed.
- **Communities:** AoPS forums (likely to frown on self-promotion, unverified; contribute solutions rather than ads), r/mathematics and r/learnmath (unverified candidates; no AMC-specific subreddit was confirmed), Discord servers for math competitions, and parent WeChat groups for the Chinese audience.
- **Risk:** AI narration and an AI-generated character may draw skepticism in a community that values solution correctness (see the comment correcting Michael Penn). Mathematical accuracy and showing multiple solutions are the credibility currency.

### Gaps
- Reddit and Discord community sizes and self-promotion rules were not retrieved.
- AoPS forum norms on linking original-problem videos were not verified.
- MATHCOUNTS video demand and channels (MATHCOUNTS' own channel, Countdown-round videos) were not researched.
