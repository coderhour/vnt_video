# The Number You Can Never Make (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's six hundred sixty-eight? Nope!

Round down x, two x and three x, and add. How many totals from one to one thousand?

Comment your letter, then watch!

From zero to one, the total goes zero, one, two, three.

At one, all three floors jump at once. Three leaps to six!

Four and five get skipped, in every block of six.

One thousand leaves remainder four. So it's out, too!

So three hundred thirty-three are skipped. Six hundred sixty-seven are left!

Six hundred sixty-eight was the trap.

Did you get six hundred sixty-seven? Tell me below!
