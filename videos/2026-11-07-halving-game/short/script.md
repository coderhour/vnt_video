# The Halving Game (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's fifty? Nope!

Take away one, or halve and round down. Whoever gets to zero wins.

From one to a hundred, how many starting numbers let player two always win?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Red means the player to move loses: two, six, eight, ten, fourteen.

But four, twelve and sixteen are even AND green! Count their twos.

Reds have an ODD number of twos. Eight has three, but four has only two.

With one two: twenty-five. With three: six. With five: two. That's thirty-three!

Fifty was the trap.

Did you get thirty-three? Tell me below!
