# A Random Frog on a Hexagon (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's twelve? Nope!

A frog hops around a hexagon, to either neighbor, with chance one half.

On average, how many jumps to visit all six corners?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Color the visited corners green: always one block, with the frog at an end.

Straighten it, with an exit on each side. The average wait is steps to one exit, times steps to the other.

So, one times four: four jumps! The stages go one, two, three, four, five!

Add them up, and you get fifteen jumps!

Twelve was the trap. The stages aren't equal!

Did you get fifteen? Tell me below!
