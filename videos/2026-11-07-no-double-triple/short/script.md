# No Doubles, No Triples (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's fifty? Nope!

Pick from one to one hundred, but none may be double or triple another.

What's the most you can pick?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Divide out all twos and threes. Numbers left with one fill this grid.

Right is double, up is triple. So clashes are neighbors!

Pair neighbors into dominoes. Each keeps at most one number.

Smaller grids hold the rest. In total, thirty-nine dominoes!

So keep at most one hundred minus thirty-nine. That's sixty-one!

Fifty was the trap. It's not the most!

Did you get sixty-one? Tell me below!
