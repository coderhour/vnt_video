# The Product That Collapses | AMC 8-Style Level 5

## Intro

Four brackets, all multiplied together. Do you really have to multiply it all out?

Most kids start multiplying, and the numbers explode. But there's a trick that makes it collapse!

Hi, I'm Vantee! Let's find out how.

## The question

Here's the question.

What is one plus one over two, times one plus one over four,

times one plus one over sixteen, times one plus one over two hundred fifty-six?

Here are the five choices. Some of these numbers are huge!

## What are we asked?

So what are we really looking for?

Four brackets, each one plus a fraction. We multiply them all.

Look at the bottom numbers: two, four, sixteen, two hundred fifty-six.

Each one is the one before it, squared!

Two squared is four. Four squared is sixteen. Sixteen squared is two hundred fifty-six.

The first bracket is three over two, and the rest are bigger than one.

So the answer is bigger than three over two.

## Solution 1: spot the pattern

Welcome back! Let's start the honest way: multiply one bracket at a time.

One plus one over two is three over two.

Times five over four. Three times five is fifteen, and two times four is eight.

So we have fifteen over eight.

Times seventeen over sixteen. Fifteen times seventeen is two hundred fifty-five.

Eight times sixteen is one hundred twenty-eight.

Now look at the tops: three, fifteen, two hundred fifty-five.

Each top is ONE less than double its bottom!

So each answer is a little under two.

Two minus one over two. Two minus one over eight. Two minus one over one hundred twenty-eight.

Now the last bracket: times two hundred fifty-seven over two hundred fifty-six.

For the new bottom, multiply one hundred twenty-eight by two hundred fifty-six.

The new top is two hundred fifty-five times two hundred fifty-seven. That's a big one!

Here's a shortcut. Write it as two hundred fifty-six minus one, times two hundred fifty-six plus one.

Multiply it out, and the middle parts cancel.

So the top is two hundred fifty-six squared, minus one.

And two hundred fifty-six squared is exactly double the new bottom.

So the top is one less than double the bottom again. The answer is C!

## Solution 2: the helper

Here's a cleverer way, with a helper.

Let's bring in a helper: one minus one over two. That's one over two.

One minus one over two, times one plus one over two. That's our shortcut again!

It gives one minus one over four.

Now one minus one over four meets one plus one over four. That makes one minus one over sixteen.

Next comes one minus one over two hundred fifty-six.

Last, one minus one over two hundred fifty-six squared.

Each step knocks over the next one. The whole chain collapses!

Hold on! We multiplied by one over two, so we must undo it. Let's double it!

Double everything: two, minus two over two hundred fifty-six squared.

That's exactly choice C again!

When would you pick this way? When each bottom is the last one squared.

## Common traps

Now let's look at the wrong answers. Each one is a trap.

A is what you get if you forget to undo the helper.

But A is less than one, and every bracket is bigger than one. It can't be right!

B stops after three brackets. Don't forget the last one!

D is two, minus ONE over two hundred fifty-six squared. The tiny piece wasn't doubled.

Choice E is two, PLUS two over two hundred fifty-six squared. That's the wrong sign!

Every bracket is bigger than one, so A is out.

And our pattern says the answer is just under two, so E is out.

## Variant: the answer

Let's check. The helper is one minus one over three. That's two over three.

The chain collapses: one minus one over nine, then one minus one over eighty-one.

That's eighty over eighty-one.

Now undo the helper. We multiplied by two over three, so we multiply by three over two.

That gives two hundred forty over one hundred sixty-two, which is forty over twenty-seven.

Doubling out of habit gives one hundred sixty over eighty-one. That's too big!

Check it the honest way: four over three, times ten over nine is forty over twenty-seven.

If you got forty over twenty-seven, amazing work!

## Recap

Here's the big idea to remember.

When each bottom is the last one squared, bring in a helper: one minus the first fraction.

Each pair turns into one minus a square, and the chain collapses. Then undo the helper!
