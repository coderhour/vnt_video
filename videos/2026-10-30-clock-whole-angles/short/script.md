# When Is the Clock Angle a Whole Number? (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's twenty-nine? Nope!

Look at every minute from one o'clock to one fifty-nine.

At how many of those times is the smaller angle between the hands a whole number of degrees?

Comment your letter, then watch!

The minute hand moves six degrees a minute. That's always a whole number.

The hour hand starts at thirty, and moves half a degree a minute.

On odd minutes, it's on a half, like thirty and a half. On even minutes, it's whole.

Whole minus a half is never whole. So the angle is whole every other minute!

Half of the sixty times. That's thirty!

Twenty-nine was the trap. It forgets one o'clock.

Did you get thirty? Tell me below!
