# When Is the Clock Angle a Whole Number? (short)

At one o'clock, the hands make thirty degrees. A nice whole number.

One minute later? Twenty-four and a half! Not whole.

Look at every minute from one o'clock to one fifty-nine.

At how many of those times is the smaller angle a whole number of degrees?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

The minute hand moves six degrees a minute. Always whole.

The hour hand moves half a degree a minute.

Even minutes: whole. Odd minutes: a half.

Whole minus a half is never whole. So every other minute works!

Half of the sixty times. That's thirty!

And one o'clock counts too.
