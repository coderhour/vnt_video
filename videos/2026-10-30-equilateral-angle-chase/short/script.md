# The Tiny Angle Inside a Triangle (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's forty? Nope!

The big triangle is equilateral. A D equals A E.

Angle B A D is twenty degrees. What is x, the angle E D C?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Chase the angles! Angle A D C is outside the left triangle. So it's sixty plus twenty, that's eighty.

Triangle A D E has two equal sides, and its top angle is forty. So each base angle is seventy.

Eighty, minus seventy, is what's left for x.

So x is just ten degrees!

Forty was the trap. That's the top angle, not the angle at D.
