# The Tiny Angle Inside a Triangle (short)

Only ONE angle is given. Can you find this tiny one?

It looks impossible. But there's a trick!

The big triangle is equilateral. A D equals A E.

Angle B A D is twenty degrees. What is x, the angle E D C?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Chase the angles! Angle A D C is outside the left triangle. So it's sixty plus twenty, that's eighty.

Inside, triangle A D E has a forty degree top and two equal sides. So each base angle is seventy.

Eighty, minus seventy, is what's left for x.

So x is ten degrees!

Look! Ten is exactly half of twenty.
