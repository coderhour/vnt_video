# AMC 10 Guide (short)

AMC ten tip! More right answers, more points? Nope!

Right earns six points, wrong earns zero, and a blank earns one point five.

Sheet A: sixteen right, nine wrong. Sheet B: fourteen right, eleven blank. Which wins?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Sheet A: sixteen times six is ninety-six.

Sheet B: fourteen times six is eighty-four. Eleven blanks add sixteen point five.

Eighty-four plus sixteen point five is one hundred point five.

Sheet B wins, with one hundred point five. That clears this season's AIME cutoff!
