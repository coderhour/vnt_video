# Isosceles Angle Chase (short)

Two isosceles triangles, one angle given.

Find the angle at the top!

A B equals A C, and B D equals B C. Angle A B D is twenty-four degrees.

What is angle A at the top?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Call the top angle a. The outside angle B D C equals a plus twenty-four.

Angle C matches it, and so does angle A B C.

All three angles of the big triangle: three a plus forty-eight equals one hundred eighty.

Three a is one hundred thirty-two. So a is forty-four degrees!

The twenty-four is the trap!
