# Isosceles Angle Chase (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's twenty-four? Nope!

A B equals A C, and B D equals B C. Angle A B D is twenty-four degrees.

What is angle A, at the top?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Call the top angle a. The outside angle at D is a plus twenty-four.

B D equals B C, so angle C is a plus twenty-four too.

And angle A B C matches angle C.

The big triangle: three a plus forty-eight is one hundred eighty.

Three a is one hundred thirty-two, so a is forty-four!
