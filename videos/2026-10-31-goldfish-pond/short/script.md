# Goldfish Pond (short)

A pond has two hundred fish, ninety percent goldfish.

Remove goldfish until eighty percent remain. Most kids say ten!

How many goldfish must go, so that eighty percent of the fish left are goldfish?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Watch the fish that stay! One hundred eighty goldfish, so twenty other fish.

Those twenty never leave. At the end they're twenty percent, one fifth of the pond.

So the whole pond is five times twenty, one hundred fish.

Two hundred fish down to one hundred. So one hundred goldfish must go!

Not ninety minus eighty. The total changes!
