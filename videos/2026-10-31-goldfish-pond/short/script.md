# Goldfish Pond (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's ten? Nope!

A pond has two hundred fish, and ninety percent are goldfish.

How many goldfish must go, so that eighty percent of the fish left are goldfish?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Watch the fish that stay! One hundred eighty goldfish means twenty other fish.

Those twenty never leave. At the end, they're twenty percent: one fifth of the pond.

So the pond ends with five times twenty, one hundred fish.

Two hundred down to one hundred. So one hundred goldfish must go!

Ten was the trap.
