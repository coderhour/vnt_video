# Perimeter 24 Triangles (short)

Three whole-number sides that add up to twenty-four.

How many different triangles? Most kids miss one or two!

How many different triangles have whole-number sides and a perimeter of twenty-four?

The same three sides count only once.

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Here's the trick. The longest side must be under twelve. That's half the perimeter.

And at least eight, or the sides can't add up to twenty-four.

So list the triangles by their longest side. Eleven, ten, nine, and eight.

Five, plus four, plus two, plus one. That's twelve triangles, choice C!

Missing one or two is the classic slip. Listing by the longest side keeps you safe!
