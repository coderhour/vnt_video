# Perimeter 24 Triangles (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's eleven? Nope!

How many triangles have whole-number sides and a perimeter of twenty-four?

Same three sides count only once.

Comment your letter, then watch!

The longest side must be under twelve. That's half of twenty-four.

Three sevens make only twenty-one. So it's at least eight.

Longest side eleven gives five. Ten gives four. Nine gives two.

And eight gives just one: eight, eight, eight!

Five plus four plus two plus one is twelve triangles!

Eleven was the trap. It leaves out the equilateral one.

Did you get twelve? Tell me below!
