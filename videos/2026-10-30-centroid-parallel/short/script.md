# Where Do the Three Medians Meet? (short)

The three medians of a triangle meet at one point, G.

Draw a line through G, parallel to the base. How long is it?

The base B C is twenty-four. E F goes through G, parallel to B C.

How long is E F?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Here's the secret. G is two thirds of the way down each median.

So shrink the whole triangle toward A, to two thirds size.

Side B C lands right on E F. Every length becomes two thirds.

Two thirds of twenty-four is sixteen!

Not twelve. G is two thirds down, not halfway.
