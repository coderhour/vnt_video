# Where Do the Three Medians Meet? (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's twelve? Nope!

The medians of triangle A B C meet at G. Side B C is twenty-four.

E F runs through G, parallel to B C. How long is E F?

Comment your letter, then watch!

G is two thirds of the way down each median, from A.

Now shrink the triangle toward A, until B C lands on E F.

The middle of B C slides up the median, and lands right on G.

So every length shrinks to two thirds.

Two thirds of twenty-four is sixteen!

Twelve was the trap. That's halfway down, not two thirds.

Did you get sixteen? Tell me below!
