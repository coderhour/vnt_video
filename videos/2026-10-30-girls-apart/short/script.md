# Keep the Girls Apart (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's five over twenty-eight? Nope!

Five boys and three girls stand in a random order.

What is the probability that no two girls stand next to each other?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Line up the five boys first. That makes six gaps, counting both ends.

Two girls in one gap would touch. So each gap gets at most one girl.

Pick three of the six gaps. That's twenty ways, out of fifty-six equally likely sets of spots.

Twenty over fifty-six is five over fourteen!

Five over twenty-eight was the trap. It forgets an end gap.
