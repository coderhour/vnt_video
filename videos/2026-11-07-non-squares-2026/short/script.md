# The 2026th Non-Square (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's two thousand seventy? Nope!

Cross out the perfect squares: two, three, five, six, seven, eight, ten.

What's the two thousand twenty-sixth number in the list?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Here's the trick. Every perfect square you cross out pushes the rest one spot along.

Ten sits in spot seven: seven, plus three squares!

Forty-five squared is two thousand twenty-five. That makes forty-five squares!

Two thousand twenty-six plus forty-five is two thousand seventy-one!

Two thousand seventy forgot that one is a square.
