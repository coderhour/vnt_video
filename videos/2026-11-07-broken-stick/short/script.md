# Broken Stick (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's one over four? Nope!

Snap a stick at two random spots: three pieces.

What's the chance the longest piece is less than twice the shortest?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Make the stick sixty long. Two cuts make one dot in the square.

Keep the top half, a mirror of the bottom. Its area is one thousand eight hundred.

Now light up every winning dot.

They make a hexagon: six triangles of thirty, so one hundred eighty!

One hundred eighty out of one thousand eight hundred. One over ten!

One over four, the triangle chance, was the trap.
