# When Does 3^n End in 01? (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's four? Nope!

Powers of three grow fast!

What is the smallest n, so three to the n ends in zero one?

Comment your letter, then watch!

The last digit loops: three, nine, seven, one. So only every fourth power ends in one.

Three to the fourth is eighty-one. To jump four more powers, multiply by eighty-one.

The tens digit drops by two. Sixty-one, forty-one, twenty-one, and then zero one!

Five jumps of four powers. So n is twenty!

Four was the trap. Eighty-one ends in one, but not zero one.
