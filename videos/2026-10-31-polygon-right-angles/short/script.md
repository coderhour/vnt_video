# Polygon Right Angles (short)

Two square corners, and every other corner is one hundred sixty degrees.

Don't add up angles. Go for a walk!

Two corners are ninety degrees. All the others are one hundred sixty.

How many sides does it have?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Walk around the outside. Your turns always add up to three hundred sixty degrees.

A square corner turns you ninety. A one hundred sixty corner turns you only twenty.

Square corners use up one hundred eighty. The rest comes in turns of twenty: nine corners!

Nine plus the two square corners is eleven sides!

Nearly flat corners take lots of sides to go around.
