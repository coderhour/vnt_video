# Polygon Right Angles (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's nine? Nope!

A convex polygon has two square corners. Every other corner is one hundred sixty degrees.

How many sides does it have?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Walk around the outside. Your turns always add up to three hundred sixty.

Square corners turn you ninety. The others turn you just twenty.

Square corners use one hundred eighty. That leaves one hundred eighty.

One hundred eighty divided by twenty is nine corners!

Nine plus the two square ones. That's eleven sides!

Nine was the trap. Don't forget the square corners!

Did you get eleven? Tell me below!
