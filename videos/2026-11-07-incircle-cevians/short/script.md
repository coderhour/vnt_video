# Where Do These Two Lines Cross? (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's two to one? Nope!

Sides thirteen, fourteen, fifteen. The circle inside touches B C at D, C A at E.

A D and B E cross at P. What is A P to P D?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Each corner's two pieces out to the circle are equal. That gives six, eight, eight, seven.

Weigh the corners so each side balances: B twenty-eight, C twenty-one, A twenty-four.

Merge B and C: forty-nine at D.

So A P to P D is forty-nine to twenty-four!

Two to one was the trap.

Did you get forty-nine to twenty-four? Tell me below!
