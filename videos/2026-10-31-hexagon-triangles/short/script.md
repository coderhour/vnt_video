# Triangles in a Hexagon (short)

This hexagon is cut into twenty-four little triangles.

But bigger ones hide inside! How many in all?

A regular hexagon is cut into twenty-four small triangles.

How many triangles of any size are in the figure?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Here's a shortcut. Count only the triangles pointing up.

Twelve little ones, six medium ones, and one big one. That's nineteen pointing up.

Now flip the hexagon upside down. It looks the same, so there are nineteen pointing down too!

Two times nineteen is thirty-eight!

Counting by size is slow. Flipping is fast.
