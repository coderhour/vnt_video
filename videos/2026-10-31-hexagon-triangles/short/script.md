# Triangles in a Hexagon (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's thirty-six? Nope!

A regular hexagon is cut into twenty-four small triangles.

How many triangles of any size are in the figure?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Here's a shortcut. Count only the triangles pointing up. Twelve little ones.

Six with side two, and one big one. That's nineteen pointing up.

Now turn the hexagon upside down. It looks the same, so nineteen triangles point down too!

Two times nineteen is thirty-eight!

Thirty-six was the trap. It misses the two big ones.
