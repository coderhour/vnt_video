# Nine Lights, How Many Patterns? (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's three hundred eighty-four? Nope!

Nine lights in a three by three grid, each on or off.

How many patterns have at least one full row or column all lit?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Count the losers: no full row or column.

Each row has seven safe choices. So seven cubed: three hundred forty-three.

Seventy-eight of those still have a full column. That leaves two hundred sixty-five losers.

All five hundred twelve, minus the losers, is two hundred forty-seven!

Three hundred eighty-four was the trap. It counts this one twice!

Did you get two hundred forty-seven? Tell me below!
