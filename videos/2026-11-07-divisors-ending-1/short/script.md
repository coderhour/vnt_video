# Which Divisors End in 1? (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's twenty-five? Nope!

Take three to the sixth, times seven to the fourth, times thirteen squared.

How many of its positive divisors end in the digit one?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Only last digits matter. Here's three to the a, times thirteen to the c.

Five boxes end in one. Powers of seven end in one, seven, nine, three, one.

A box ending in one works with two of them. Every other box works with exactly one!

Five times two, plus sixteen. That's twenty-six!

Twenty-five was the trap. Seven doesn't end in one!
