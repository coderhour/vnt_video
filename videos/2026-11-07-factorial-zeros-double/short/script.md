# Double n, Double the Zeros? (short)

AMC ten challenge! Think it's twenty-four? Nope!

For how many n up to one hundred does the factorial of two n end in exactly twice as many zeros as n factorial?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Zeros come from fives. So write n in base five.

Eight in base five is one, three. Double it: the three makes six, and that carries an extra five!

So every digit must be zero, one or two. Three times three times three is twenty-seven.

Drop zero, zero, zero, and that's twenty-six!

Twenty-four was the trap. One and two count!
