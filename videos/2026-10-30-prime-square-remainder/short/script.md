# Why Do Prime Squares Always Leave 1? (short)

Pick any prime bigger than three. Square it, add seventeen.

Divide by twelve, and the remainder is ALWAYS the same!

Let p be a prime bigger than three. Divide p squared plus seventeen by twelve.

What's the remainder?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Take seven. Seven squared is forty-nine dots.

Remove a corner dot, and slide the bottom row to the side.

Now it's a rectangle: six by eight. The neighbors of seven!

One neighbor is a multiple of six, and the other is even.

So p squared is a multiple of twelve, plus one.

One plus seventeen is eighteen. The remainder is six!

Always six, for every prime bigger than three.
