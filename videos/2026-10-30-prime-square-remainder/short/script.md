# Why Do Prime Squares Always Leave 1? (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's one? Nope!

p is a prime bigger than three. Divide p squared plus seventeen by twelve.

What's the remainder?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Take seven. Seven squared is forty-nine dots.

Remove a corner dot, and slide the bottom row to the side.

Now it's six by eight. The neighbors of seven!

One neighbor is a multiple of six, and the other is even.

So p squared is a multiple of twelve, plus one.

One plus seventeen is eighteen. That leaves six!

One is the trap. It forgets the seventeen.
