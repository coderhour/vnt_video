# Big Triangle, Minus a Small One (short)

Find the area of this shaded shape.

Lots of kids answer forty-eight. But that's NOT quite right!

The rectangle is twelve by eight, and M is the midpoint of the top.

What is the area of the shaded shape A P M D?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Here's the trick. Start with a bigger triangle, A C D. It's half the rectangle.

But our shape misses a small corner, triangle P C M.

Similar triangles tell us its height is eight over three. So its area is eight.

Forty-eight minus eight is forty!

Forty-eight forgets the little corner.
