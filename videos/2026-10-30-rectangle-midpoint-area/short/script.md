# Big Triangle, Minus a Small One (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's forty-eight? Nope!

The rectangle is twelve by eight, and M is the midpoint of the top.

What's the area of the shaded shape, A P M D?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Start with the big triangle A C D. It's half the rectangle: forty-eight.

But it misses one corner, triangle P C M.

It's half as wide as the triangle below it, so half as tall: eight over three.

Six times eight over three is sixteen. Half of that is eight.

Forty-eight minus eight is forty!

Forty-eight was the trap. It keeps the corner.
