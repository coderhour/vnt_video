# Seven-Point Star (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's one hundred eighty? Nope!

Seven evenly spaced points, each joined to the second point after it.

What do the seven tip angles add up to?

Comment your letter, then watch!

Walk a pencil around the star. At each tip, the tip and the turn make one hundred eighty.

Watch it go all the way around. The pencil circles the center twice!

Two circles is seven hundred twenty degrees of turning.

Seven times one hundred eighty, minus seven hundred twenty. That's five hundred forty degrees!

One hundred eighty was the trap. That's the five-point star.
