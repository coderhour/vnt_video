# Seven-Point Star (short)

Add up the tips of this star, without measuring a single angle!

All you need is a pencil.

Seven evenly spaced points, each joined to the second point after it.

What do the seven tip angles add up to?

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Walk a pencil around the star. At every tip, the tip and the turn make one hundred eighty.

Watch it go all the way around. The pencil circles the center twice!

Two circles is seven hundred twenty degrees of turning.

Seven times one hundred eighty is one thousand two hundred sixty.

So the tips get the rest: five hundred forty degrees!

Not one hundred eighty. That's the five-point star.
