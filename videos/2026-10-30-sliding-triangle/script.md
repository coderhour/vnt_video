# Slide a Triangle, Find the Shaded Part | AMC 8-Style Level 4

## Intro

Take a triangle. Copy it. Slide the copy a little to the right.

How much of the copied triangle sticks out? Lots of kids answer twenty-four here.

Hi, I'm Vantee! Let's find out what's really going on.

## The question

Here's the question. A right triangle has legs twelve and nine.

The triangle is copied, and the copy is slid four to the right, as shown.

What is the area of the shaded part of the copy, the part that does not overlap the original?

Here are the five choices. Take a good look.

## What are we asked?

So what do we know?

The bottom leg is twelve, and the left leg is nine. The square corner is at the bottom left.

The copy slides four to the right, along the bottom line.

We want the shaded part: the copy, minus the piece both triangles share.

## Solution 1: whole minus overlap

Welcome back! Our strategy: find the whole copy, then take away the overlap.

The copy is the same triangle. To get its area, we take one over two, times twelve, times nine.

That's fifty-four. So the whole copy is fifty-four.

Now look at the overlap. It's a triangle too!

Its square corner is where the copy's left side stands, four along the bottom.

Its bottom runs from there to the end of the original. That's twelve minus four, which is eight.

Its slanted side lies along the original's long side. So it has the same slant.

A square corner and the same slant mean the same angles. It's a smaller copy of the original.

Same shape, smaller size. Eight out of twelve is two over three.

So its height is two over three of nine. That's six.

The overlap's area is one over two, times eight, times six. That's twenty-four.

Now take it away. Fifty-four minus twenty-four is thirty.

The shaded part is thirty! The answer is E.

## Solution 2: the left-over strip

Let's try another way. Look at the original triangle this time.

It loses the same overlap. And it has the same area, fifty-four.

So the part of the original left over is the SAME size as the shaded part!

That remaining part is the strip on the left, four wide.

Its left side is nine tall. How tall is its right side?

The long side drops nine as it goes twelve across. That's three for every four.

So after four across, it has dropped three. Nine minus three is six.

The strip is a trapezoid, with parallel sides nine and six, and width four.

A trapezoid's area is the average of its two parallel sides, times the width.

Nine plus six is fifteen. Half is seven and a half. Times four is thirty!

Same answer! When would you pick this way? When the strip is easier to see than the overlap.

## Common traps

Now, let's look at the wrong answers. Each one is a trap.

Twenty-four is the overlap. It answers the wrong question. We wanted the part outside it.

Twenty-seven is half of fifty-four. But nothing in the picture cuts the copy in half.

Eighteen is what you'd get if you called the strip a triangle, base four, height nine.

But the strip's right side is six tall. A triangle would come to a point there.

Twenty-eight is what you'd get if the long side dropped four, not three, for every four across.

Then the right side would be five, and the trapezoid would give twenty-eight.

So here's a great habit. Ask what the shaded part is, then check every height.

## New slide: the answer

Let's check. The whole triangle's area is one over two, times ten, times five. That's twenty-five.

The overlap's bottom is ten minus four, which is six. Six out of ten is three over five.

Its height is three over five of five. That's three.

The overlap is one over two, times six, times three. That's nine.

Twenty-five minus nine is sixteen!

Check with the strip. Its sides are five and three, and it's four wide.

Five plus three is eight. Half is four. Four times four is sixteen. It matches!

If you found sixteen, amazing work!

## Recap

Here's the big idea to remember.

Slide a triangle along its base, and the overlap is a smaller copy of the same shape.

Find the whole, take away the overlap, and you have the part left over.
