# Four Triangles in a Trapezoid (short)

Two diagonals cut a trapezoid into four triangles. We know just ONE area!

Top six, bottom ten, shaded triangle nine. What's the whole area?

Here are the choices.

Pause and try it!

*(Pause and try it: 3 s countdown)*

Top and bottom are copies, sides six and ten: a three to five ratio.

Areas grow by the ratio twice. Nine times five over three, times five over three, is twenty-five.

The side triangles share the top one's height. Nine times five over three: fifteen each.

Now add: nine, twenty-five, fifteen, fifteen.

The trapezoid has area sixty-four!

That's choice D.
