# Four Triangles in a Trapezoid (short)

AMC eight challenge! Think it's fifty-four? Nope!

Top six, bottom ten, and the top triangle is nine. What's the whole area?

Comment your letter, then watch!

The bottom triangle is a copy of the top one. Six to ten is three to five.

The base grows by five over three, and so does the height. So nine becomes twenty-five!

The side triangles share the top one's height. Nine times five over three: fifteen each.

Nine plus twenty-five plus fifteen plus fifteen is sixty-four!

Fifty-four was the trap. It grows the area only once.
